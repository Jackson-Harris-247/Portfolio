Winning the CERN BL4S competition

Published: June 5, 2026 - Jackson Harris

Source Code Here

The Cern Beamline For Schools competition, or BL4S, is an annual event where high school students are given the oppurtunity to design and propose a scientific experiment utilizing the facilities at CERN. My group spent a couple weeks brainstorming various ideas for this years competition (2026). We had some cool plans like building our own particle detector, proposing beam dump materials and even designing a ML based anomaly detector framework. I personally really liked the anomaly detector as it would allow us to involve a lot of really cool ground up math and design a ML system. Ultimately we decided on particle beam dump materials.

Reading through literature was a large chunk of the joy of this project
